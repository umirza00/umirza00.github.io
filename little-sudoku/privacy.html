Privacy Policy

Kidz Sudoku · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Summary: Kidz Sudoku does not collect or share any personal data. Game progress, settings and the optional child profiles are kept only on your device. The app does not connect to the internet and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

1. Who we are

Kidz Sudoku is developed by Umut Mirza, an independent developer. Contact: mirzaumt@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

None. The app does not collect names, email addresses, location, photos, audio, contacts, device identifiers or any other personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in. The optional nickname a parent can give a child profile (see section 3) stays on the device and is never sent anywhere.

3. Information stored only on the device

To work, the game stores the following only on your device: game progress, earned stickers, settings (such as sound, language and session limit), how recent puzzles went (for example whether help was used) so the next puzzles can match your child, and play dates and active play time (for the rest reminder and the optional session limit).

Child profiles. Up to four children can play on one device. Each profile is a colour, an age group (3 to 9 years; no date of birth) and, only if a parent types one in the parent area, a short nickname (up to 12 characters). The nickname is shown under the child's colour on the "who is playing" screen and in the parent area. It is kept only on the device: it is never sent to us or anyone else, never spoken by the app's voice (VoiceOver reads it only if you turn VoiceOver on) and never written into file names or logs. Each profile's progress and settings are stored separately.

None of this is ever sent to us or to anyone else, but it can be part of your own iCloud or computer backup of the device. In the parent area, "Reset progress" deletes one child's progress, stickers and play history; "Reset settings" restores one child's default settings; "Delete profile" removes one child's profile and all of its data; "Delete all data" removes every profile and setting and returns the app to its first launch. Deleting the app also removes everything.

4. Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs and no advertising, analytics, tracking or social media components. No data is shared with or sold to anyone.

5. Children's privacy

Kidz Sudoku is made for children and is designed for the App Store Kids Category. Because no personal data is collected, no personal information of children is processed by us under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) or similar laws. Child profiles and their optional nicknames exist only on your device and are managed by you; we never receive them. Creating, editing and deleting profiles, the parent area (progress, settings, parent corner) and the only external link in the app (this policy) are behind a parental gate. The app contains no purchases or advertising.

6. If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and message only to answer you. We do not share them and delete them when they are no longer needed. You can ask for access to or deletion of this correspondence at mirzaumt@gmail.com.

7. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses under its own privacy statement. The website uses no cookies or analytics of its own.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
