Privacy Policy

Little Sudoku · Last updated: November 10, 2026

Summary: Little Sudoku does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app does not connect to the internet and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

1. Who we are

Little Sudoku is developed by Umut Mirza, an independent developer. Contact: mirzaumt@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

None. The app does not ask for or collect names, email addresses, location, photos, audio, contacts, device identifiers or any other personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

3. Information stored only on the device

To work, the game stores the following only on your device: game progress, earned stickers, settings (such as sound, language and session limit) and how recent puzzles went (for example whether help was used), so the next puzzles can match your child. This information is never sent off the device. Deleting the app, or using "Reset progress" in the parent area, removes it.

4. Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs and no advertising, analytics, tracking or social media components. No data is shared with or sold to anyone.

5. Children's privacy

Little Sudoku is made for children and is designed for the App Store Kids Category. Because no personal data is collected, no personal information of children is processed under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) or similar laws. Settings and the only external link (this policy) are behind a parental gate.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: mirzaumt@gmail.com
