Privacy Policy

Kidz Sudoku · Last updated: November 10, 2026

Summary: Kidz Sudoku does not collect or share any personal data. Game progress and settings are kept only on your device. The app does not connect to the internet and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

1. Who we are

Kidz Sudoku is developed by Umut Mirza, an independent developer. Contact: mirzaumt@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

None. The app does not ask for or collect names, email addresses, location, photos, audio, contacts, device identifiers or any other personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

3. Information stored only on the device

To work, the game stores the following only on your device: game progress, earned stickers, settings (such as sound, language and session limit) how recent puzzles went (for example whether help was used) so the next puzzles can match your child, and play dates and active play time (for the rest reminder and the optional session limit). It is never sent to us or to anyone else, but it can be part of your own iCloud or computer backup of the device. "Reset progress" in the parent area deletes progress, stickers and play history; "Reset settings" restores the default settings; deleting the app removes everything.

4. Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs and no advertising, analytics, tracking or social media components. No data is shared with or sold to anyone.

5. Children's privacy

Kidz Sudoku is made for children and is designed for the App Store Kids Category. Because no personal data is collected, no personal information of children is processed under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) or similar laws. The parent area (progress, settings, parent corner) and the only external link in the app (this policy) are behind a parental gate. The app contains no purchases or advertising.

6. If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and message only to answer you. We do not share them and delete them when they are no longer needed. You can ask for access to or deletion of this correspondence at mirzaumt@gmail.com.

7. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses under its own privacy statement. The website uses no cookies or analytics of its own.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: mirzaumt@gmail.com
