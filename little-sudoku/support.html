Frequently asked questions

How do I open the parent area?
Tap the parent icon on the home screen and answer the multiplication question written in words.

How do I change the language?
Parent area → Settings → Language. The game supports English, Turkish, Spanish, Polish, Russian and Kazakh.

How do I reset progress?
Parent area → Settings → Reset progress.

Can I limit play time?
Yes. In the parent area, choose a 10, 15 or 20 minute session limit. It takes effect after your child finishes the current puzzle.

Does it cost anything or show ads?
No. Little Sudoku is completely free, with no ads, purchases or subscriptions.

Do you collect data?
No. See the privacy policy.
